The ritual of fragrance

The art of wear.
Lasting presence.

Mastering the ritual of application transforms fine perfume from a simple scent into an unforgettable personal aura.

01

Hydrate

Moisturize skin with an unscented lotion to create an optimal canvas that anchors fragrance molecules.

02

Pulse Points

Spray delicately on warmth zones—wrists, clavicles, inner elbows, and nape of the neck.

03

Never Rub

Allow the fragrance to dry down naturally; friction crushes delicate top notes and disturbs the composition.

04

Layer Accords

Pair with matching hair mist or scented body balm for a multi-dimensional, enveloping sillage.

05

Fabric Mist

Gently mist natural cashmere, silk lapels, or scarves for an enchanting trail that lingers for days.
